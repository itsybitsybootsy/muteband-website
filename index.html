We don't block sound.
We confuse the mic.

A physical quirk in every modern microphone, exploited in three phases.

Phase 01

Emit

25 – 40 kHz · ultrasonic

Pulses above human hearing. You hear nothing. Mics get hit at full strength.

Phase 02

Saturate

non-linear MEMS response

MEMS sensors fold the ultrasound back into the audible band as noise. The mic deafens itself.

Phase 03

Destroy

recording corrupted at source

Every recording is noise. Filters can't restore it. Transcription fails. Voice cloning fails.
